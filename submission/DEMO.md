## What the video shows

1. **The problem** — a perfect Primavera plan, but the site is a week ahead of what the plan knows.
2. **The solution** — SETU as the real-time bridge between site and plan.
3. **Multilingual, voice-first capture** — a supervisor sends a voice note / text (Hindi/Hinglish/English) via Telegram; a diary page is photographed and read.
4. **The live moment** — the schedule/Gantt updates within a second, no laptop touched; medium-confidence reports land in the review inbox.
5. **Trust** — one-click approve, the tamper-evident receipt behind every date.
6. **Ask your project + institutional memory**, then the **P6-importable export**.
